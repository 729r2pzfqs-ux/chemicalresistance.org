Not Recommended For

Acetal (POM) may degrade when exposed to:

  • Strong acids (sulfuric, nitric, HCl)
  • Strong oxidizers
  • Phenol
  • Chlorinated solvents at elevated temperatures

Tip: POM is attacked by strong acids. For acid resistance, use HDPE, PP, or PTFE.
