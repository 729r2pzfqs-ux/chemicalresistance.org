Not Recommended For

LDPE may degrade when exposed to:

  • Strong oxidizing acids
  • Chlorinated solvents
  • Aromatic hydrocarbons
  • Temperatures above 80°C

Tip: LDPE is less rigid than HDPE. For structural applications, use HDPE or PP.
